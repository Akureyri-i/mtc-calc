# MTC Calculator 0.1.0

The first public version. Download `MTC-Calculator.exe` below, start Roblox and Multicrew Tank
Combat, run it, and take an artillery seat. 中文说明见下方。

## What's in it

- **Firing solutions on the full map.** Mark targets as usual; the plan panel beside the map lists
  each one with its distance, charge and flight time, and the selected one with its low and high
  arcs, height, azimuth and a terrain profile.
- **Gunner panel and aim strip** next to the game's angle readout: the target's angles and how far
  you still have to move.
- **Charge dots in the loading menu**: green where a charge reaches the selected target, red where
  it does not, and the charge to use on the panel.
- **Terrain check.** A hill or building in the way switches to the high arc or another charge;
  trees and small houses only get a warning.
- **Predicted landing point** on the map, and each shell's actual landing is spotted.
- Finds the map and your gun by itself, and works at any window size and Windows display scaling.
- Downloads map pictures, heightmaps, ammunition data and terrain layers by itself, and tries
  again if a download fails.
- Optional anonymous statistics, off unless you agree; problem reports you make and send yourself.
  See [PRIVACY.md](https://github.com/Akureyri-i/mtc-calc/blob/main/PRIVACY.md).

## Good to know

- Windows shows "Windows protected your PC" the first time, because the program is not signed.
  Click *More info*, then *Run anyway*.
- The calculator has no window outside the game. Its icon is in the notification area at the
  bottom right; right-click it to quit.
- Where the shell leaves the barrel was measured in game for the M109, M109A6, 2S33, D-30,
  Centurion AVRE and the 60 mm mortar. Other guns borrow the closest one, so their shells can land
  several metres from the prediction (6 to 8 m where we checked).

- [VirusTotal report](https://www.virustotal.com/gui/file/b97d2d45d11f9a76aae2f3b1bc691a69e7eec14a26964fd6834436d2d8778ac4): 1 of 70 engines flags it, with a low machine-learning score (Trapmine); the other 69 find nothing.

## Known issues

- Target marks jitter a little while you drag the full map.
- On Zone 11 the full map is matched less reliably, and targets can flicker.
- The heightmap shows trees by their crowns, so dense woods can still count as ground.
- At 1920×1080 it uses about twice the CPU it does at 2560×1440 (around 0.3 of one core).
- Near the edge of a charge's range the suggested charge can switch back and forth.
- Standing on your own empty vehicle's map icon after getting out, the gun position may not
  update.

Not in this version: a manual mode without the map, salvos and corrections, and counting the
rounds left.

## 中文

第一个公开版本。下载下面的 `MTC-Calculator.exe`，打开 Roblox 进入 Multicrew Tank Combat，运行它，坐上火炮就行。

### 有什么

- **大地图上出射击方案。** 照常标点，地图旁的计划面板列出每个目标的距离、装药和飞行时间；选中的目标还有高低两条弹道、高差、方位和地形剖面。
- **炮手面板和指示条**贴在游戏的角度读数旁边，显示目标角度和还差多少要转。
- **装填菜单里的装药红绿点**：绿点能打到选中的目标，红点打不到；面板上直接告诉你该用哪档。
- **地形检查。** 被山坡或建筑挡住时自动换高弹道或别的装药；树和小房子只提示。
- 在地图上画出**预测落点**，并认出每发炮弹实际落在哪里。
- 自己认出地图和你的炮位，任何窗口大小、任何 Windows 缩放都能用。
- 地图图片、高度图、弹药数据和地形层都自动下载，下载失败会自动重试。
- 可选的匿名统计数据，不点同意就不发；问题报告由你自己生成、自己发送。详见[隐私说明](https://github.com/Akureyri-i/mtc-calc/blob/main/PRIVACY.zh-CN.md)。

### 使用前须知

- 第一次运行时 Windows 会提示“已保护你的电脑”，因为程序没有签名。点“更多信息”，再点“仍要运行”。
- 计算器在游戏外没有窗口，图标在屏幕右下角的通知区，右键可以退出。
- M109、M109A6、2S33、D-30、Centurion AVRE 和 60 mm 迫击炮的出膛位置在游戏里实测过，其余的炮借用最接近的那一门，落点可能偏离预测好几米（我们核对过的是 6 到 8 米）。

### 核对下载的文件

- `MTC-Calculator.exe` 的 SHA-256：`b97d2d45d11f9a76aae2f3b1bc691a69e7eec14a26964fd6834436d2d8778ac4`
- [VirusTotal 报告](https://www.virustotal.com/gui/file/b97d2d45d11f9a76aae2f3b1bc691a69e7eec14a26964fd6834436d2d8778ac4)：70 家引擎里 1 家（Trapmine）给了“低度可疑”的机器学习打分，其余 69 家都没有报。

### 已知问题

- 拖动大地图时，目标标注会轻微抖动。
- Zone 11 的大地图对准没那么稳，目标可能时有时无。
- 高度图里的树只有树冠，密林有时仍会被当成地形。
- 1920×1080 下的 CPU 占用大约是 2560×1440 的两倍（约 0.3 个核心）。
- 在某档装药的射程边缘，推荐装药可能来回切换。
- 下车后站在自己的空车图标上时，炮位可能不更新。

这个版本还没有：不用地图的手动模式、齐射和校射、剩余弹数。
