# MTC Calculator 0.1.5

Quit the old one from its notification area icon first, then run the new one in its place. 中文说明见下方。

- **`MTC-Calculator.exe`** (about 10 MB): downloads the map pictures and heightmaps on first use, each map usable as soon as it arrives.

## New

- **Pick the shell, charge or gun yourself when it is read wrong or not at all.** Click the SHELL line on the gunner's panel or on the plan panel beside the full map: a list of the gun's shells with the game's icons, and "Auto" to go back. Your pick stays with that ammo slot until you choose Auto or change vehicle. If the gun itself wasn't recognised, the list starts with the guns (same calibre first, with the vehicles that carry them). The CHARGE line (or a charge box on the plan panel) sets this shot's charge.
- **Ammunition data now comes inside both downloads**, so the calculator works without reaching the wiki data service (it often couldn't from mainland China and then showed no panel).
- **Private server debug map:** choose *Debug map* under *Map* in the settings. It switches back to *Auto* by itself once a real map is recognised.
- **Problem reports are also saved as one zip**, selected in the folder that opens: drag that single file into a chat or an issue.

## Fixed

- **The HUD blinked in third person, and in the scope the aim indicator lagged until you moved the gun** (at some window sizes and scalings, for example 2560×1440 at 125% full screen). The calculator now measures the angle readout's text size on your screen when its usual rule reads it poorly.
- **With Roblox's Text Size one step up, the angles, the gun name and the ammo cards are read again.** Two or three steps up, the game cuts its own text short and the calculator can't read it: keep Text Size at the default or one step up.
- **The RSZO (Katyusha) rockets and the RBT-5 torpedo were never recognised**, so the shell stayed "none known". Their names have no calibre in them, which the calculator required.
- **A charge that wasn't read showed the one remembered from before.** It now shows as unknown until it is read, and the solutions use the suggested charge meanwhile.
- **The plan panel beside the full map kept one size on every screen**, running past the map on smaller screens and looking small on large ones. It now scales with the map.
- With slow downloads the map pictures were only used after all of them had arrived; now each one is used as soon as it arrives.

## Statistics

If you send statistics, the calculator asks once more at the next start: the records now also hold the shells, charges and guns you pick by hand, and what it had read for them. See [PRIVACY.md](https://github.com/Akureyri-i/mtc-calc/blob/main/PRIVACY.md).

## Please check your game settings

- In the game's settings, set the GUNNER section to: Align action bar Center, Action bar color #F0B74A, Hide minimap when aiming OFF, Weapon selection layout Horizontal, Keep action bar expanded while loading ON.
- Turn off Roblox's automatic translation and keep the game in English.
- Don't use a font pack that replaces the game's fonts. Keep Roblox's Text Size at the default or one step up.

## Checking the downloads

- SHA-256 of `MTC-Calculator.exe`: `19a73f43faae95ff99dfcdc52be32f5b80cbcce50f5b6e869dcf07f29945dcd9`

## Known issues

- With Roblox's Text Size raised, the view (minimap, full map, scope) is sometimes not recognised.
- Target marks jitter a little while you drag the full map.
- On Zone 11 the full map is matched less reliably, and targets can flicker.
- The heightmap shows trees by their crowns, so dense woods can still count as ground.
- Near the edge of a charge's range the suggested charge can switch back and forth.
- At 1920×1080 it uses about twice the CPU it does at 2560×1440 (around 0.3 of one core).
- Standing on your own empty vehicle's map icon after getting out, the gun position may not
  update.

Not in this version: a manual mode without the map, salvos and corrections, and counting the
rounds left.

## 中文

先在通知区图标上右键退出旧版，再用新版替换：

- **`MTC-Calculator.exe`**（约 10 MB）：地图图片和高度图第一次用时下载，下好一张就能用一张。

### 新增

- **弹种、装药、炮认错或认不出时可以自己选。** 点炮手面板或大地图旁方案面板上的 SHELL 一行，会列出这门炮的弹种（带游戏里的图标），选 “Auto” 恢复自动。选的记在那一格上，直到选 Auto 或换车。炮没认出来时，列表先列炮（同口径的在前，右边写车名）。点 CHARGE 一行（方案面板上点装药方框）可以改这一发的装药。
- **弹药数据两个下载都带上了**，连不上 wiki 数据服务也能用（大陆经常连不上，之前就不出面板）。
- **私人服的调试地图：** 在设置的 *Map* 里选 *Debug map*，认出真实地图后会自己切回 *Auto*。
- **问题报告同时存成一个 zip**，打开的文件夹里已经选中它，直接把这一个文件拖进 QQ 或 Issue。

### 修复

- **第三人称时 HUD 一闪一闪，开镜后指示条要动一下炮才更新**（部分窗口尺寸和缩放下，比如 2560×1440 125% 全屏）。现在常规规则读不好角度读数时，计算器会在你屏幕上量读数的字号。
- **Roblox 的“文本大小”调大一格时，角度、炮名和弹药卡片又能读了。** 调大两格、三格时游戏自己把字截短，计算器读不出来：文本大小请保持默认或调大一格。
- **RSZO（喀秋莎）的火箭和 RBT-5 的鱼雷一直认不出**，弹种一直是 none known。它们的名字里没有口径，原来必须有口径才认。
- **装药没读出来时显示的是上一次记住的。** 现在没读出来就显示未知，读到为止；这期间诸元按推荐装药给。
- **大地图旁的方案面板在所有屏幕上都是一个尺寸**，小屏幕上伸出地图、大屏幕上显得很小。现在跟着地图缩放。
- 网速慢时地图图片要全部下完才用，现在下好一张就用一张。
