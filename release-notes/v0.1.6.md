# MTC Calculator 0.1.6

Download `MTC-Calculator.exe` below. To update, quit the old version from its notification area icon first, then run the new one in its place. 中文说明见下方。

## New

- **Chinese interface.** Switch between English and 中文 at the top right of the settings window. Until you choose, it follows the Windows display language.
- **Squad markers.** When several squad members place markers, the top of the plan panel shows each letter with its number of markers; click a letter to follow those markers. Clicking any member's marker on the full map selects it. Placing a marker of your own, or a new round, goes back to your own letter.
- **Problem reports show what went wrong.** With *Screenshots* ticked, a report now also includes pictures from the recent moments the calculator failed to read the angle readout, the map view or a loading, so a problem that comes and goes is in the report even if it reads fine at the moment you create it.
- **Problem reports from the notification area icon.** Right-click the icon and choose *Create problem report*, also when the panel does not appear.

## Fixed

- **With Windows HDR on, the angles and the gun name were not read** (the capture came out overexposed). The calculator now captures HDR displays in 16-bit and converts them back.
- **On graphics cards limited to Direct3D 10, the calculator stopped with a capture error.** It now captures there too.
- **The RBT-5's torpedo was not recognised**: the game shows it as "420 mm Rocket Launcher".
- **The AVRE's shell stayed unknown at 100% and 125% scaling.** For guns whose shells fly the same way at the loaded charge, the shell is now taken from the data when the card cannot be read.
- **With Roblox's Text Size one step up, the minimap and the full map were sometimes not recognised.**
- **A marker dragged under your own vehicle icon cannot be picked up again in the game, but the calculator moved its mark with the mouse.** It now follows the game: when the game does not pick the marker up, the mark stays where it is.

## Statistics

If you send statistics, the calculator asks once more at the next start: each shot record now also holds how the angle readout moved just before the shot, seat changes and UPDATE GUN presses since the previous shot, how far the gun moved, how far the gun position used by the firing solution was from the gun, the selected target and the solution's angles, and landings near the line of fire that the prediction did not expect. See [PRIVACY.md](https://github.com/Akureyri-i/mtc-calc/blob/main/PRIVACY.md).

## 中文

请下载下方的 `MTC-Calculator.exe`。更新时先在通知区域图标的右键菜单中退出旧版本，再运行新版本替换即可。

### 新增

- **中文界面。** 在设置窗口右上角切换 English 和中文；未设置时跟随 Windows 显示语言。
- **小队标记。** 小队中有多名成员标点时，计划面板顶部显示各字母及其标点数，点击字母即可跟随该成员的标点；在大地图上点击任意成员的标点也可直接选中。自己放下标点或开始新一局时，恢复跟随自己的字母。
- **问题报告附带识别失败时的画面。** 勾选“附截图”时，报告还会包含最近几次未能识别角度读数、地图视图或装填时的画面。时有时无的问题，即使生成报告的那一刻识别正常，也能记录在报告中。
- **通知区域图标可生成问题报告。** 右键单击图标选择“生成问题报告”，面板未显示时同样可用。

### 修复

- **开启 Windows HDR 时无法读取角度和炮名**（截图过曝）。现在 HDR 显示器改用 16 位截图并换算回正常亮度。
- **显卡仅支持 Direct3D 10 时，程序报采集错误后退出。** 现在可以正常采集。
- **RBT-5 的鱼雷无法识别**：游戏中显示为“420 mm Rocket Launcher”。
- **在 100% 和 125% 缩放下，AVRE 的弹种一直显示未知。** 当前装药下各弹种弹道相同的火炮，在卡片无法读取时直接按数据确定弹种。
- **Roblox“文本大小”调大一格时，有时无法识别小地图和大地图。**
- **标点被拖到自己的车辆图标下后，游戏中无法再拖动，但计算器的标记仍随鼠标移动。** 现在与游戏保持一致：游戏未拿起标点时，计算器的标记保持不动。

### 统计数据

已选择发送统计数据的玩家，下次启动时会再次被询问：每发炮弹的记录新增了开火前角度读数的变化、与上一发之间更换座位和点击“更新炮位”的次数、火炮移动的距离、射击方案所用炮位与火炮的距离、选中的目标及射击方案给出的角度，以及落在射线附近但与预测不符的落点。详见 [PRIVACY.zh-CN.md](https://github.com/Akureyri-i/mtc-calc/blob/main/PRIVACY.zh-CN.md)。
